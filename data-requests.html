Data and privacy requests

To ask about information associated with your Rumbo conversation, email hola@rumboauto.com.

What you may request

  • access to information, where applicable;
  • correction of inaccurate information, where applicable;
  • deletion of information; or
  • a stop to further Rumbo messaging.

Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant Rumbo interaction. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will review requests under applicable law and may retain limited records when reasonably necessary for security, legal, or recordkeeping obligations.

For a messaging opt-out, you may also reply STOP or tell us you no longer want messages. Rumbo will treat a clear request as an opt-out from further automated messages unless you later complete an approved resumption process.
