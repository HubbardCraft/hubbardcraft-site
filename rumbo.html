Rumbo

Rumbo is an automotive customer-engagement technology platform developed and operated by HubbardCraft LLC. It is designed to help participating automotive businesses manage customer inquiries and guide customers toward appropriate next steps under business-defined controls.

Rumbo is in active development. Supported business-messaging channels may include WhatsApp only where officially authorized. Production WhatsApp integration and automated customer messaging are not currently represented here as live public features.

What Rumbo is designed to support

Receiving customer inquiries and managing durable customer conversations.

Collecting customer-stated preferences and readiness information to support a helpful conversation.

Coordinating customer follow-up and routing customers toward appropriate human or participating-dealer next steps.

Using AI-assisted engagement under business-defined authority controls, with human handoff where appropriate.

Customer information and controls

Rumbo is designed to handle customer information only for requested automotive communications and related customer-engagement services. Business messaging data is used for those customer-engagement purposes and is subject to access controls and business-defined automation rules.

Rumbo is designed so sensitive credit information remains outside ordinary AI conversation context. Where a conversation requires human attention, customers may be handed to a human operator or another appropriate next step.

What Rumbo does not do

  • Rumbo is not a lender and does not approve or deny credit.
  • Rumbo does not promise financing, vehicle availability, or dealer terms without authoritative business information.
  • Rumbo does not claim to be a Meta partner or to have any messaging permission before it is officially granted.

Availability and features may vary by participating business and applicable authorization. Rumbo is not represented as an operating multi-dealer network.
