Rumbo Privacy Policy V1

Proposed operator: HubbardCraft LLC (“HubbardCraft,” “we,” “us”). HubbardCraft develops and operates Rumbo, an automotive customer-engagement technology service. This draft describes the intended handling of information when Rumbo is made available through participating automotive businesses or supported business-messaging channels.

Information covered

Rumbo may process information that a customer or participating business voluntarily provides or makes available for an automotive customer-engagement request, including:

  • contact details and communication preferences;
  • messages and other business/customer interaction records;
  • vehicle interest, customer-stated preferences, and readiness information;
  • information a participating business provides to support the requested interaction; and
  • technical or service information reasonably needed to secure, operate, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

Rumbo is not intended to collect Social Security numbers, bank-account numbers, raw consumer-report information, or other highly sensitive credit data through ordinary conversation. If a customer voluntarily sends sensitive information in a normal conversation, Rumbo is designed to limit its use in ordinary AI context, avoid unnecessary repetition, and route the interaction for appropriate handling.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide and support requested automotive communications and customer-engagement services; maintain conversation continuity; respond to customer questions; coordinate follow-up; route a conversation to an appropriate human or participating business; protect the service; and comply with applicable law. Customer-stated preferences are treated as statements by the customer, not as credit, underwriting, or financing determinations.

AI-assisted processing and human review

Rumbo may use AI-assisted tools under business-defined controls to help understand messages, prepare proposed responses, identify customer-stated preferences, and recommend next actions. Rumbo’s intended design keeps operational authority in business controls and permits human review or handoff where appropriate. AI-assisted processing does not itself approve or deny credit, guarantee financing, or guarantee vehicle availability.

Service providers and participating businesses

We may use service providers that process information on our behalf to operate, secure, host, support, or improve Rumbo. We may share information with a participating automotive business when that is needed to provide the customer-requested service or an appropriate next step, subject to applicable law and business controls. We do not sell sensitive credit information.

Business messaging and WhatsApp

Where Rumbo uses supported business-messaging channels, including the WhatsApp Business Platform where officially authorized, message data may be handled to provide the relevant customer-engagement service and in accordance with applicable platform terms and policies. This draft does not represent that a production WhatsApp integration is currently live or authorized.

Credit and prequalification boundary

A future secure credit or prequalification process, if offered, would be separate from ordinary Rumbo conversation processing and subject to its own notices, consents, provider requirements, and applicable law. Rumbo does not represent in this draft that such a service is currently available.

Security, retention, and choices

We intend to use reasonable administrative, technical, and access-control measures appropriate to the service. Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, to meet legal or operational requirements, and to resolve disputes. Customers may contact us to request information about their data or available choices; requests will be handled as required by applicable law and may depend on the customer’s relationship with a participating business.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@hubbardcraft.com. If this policy becomes effective, material changes will be posted with an updated effective date.
